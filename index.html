About

Paul has made 100+ investments and served on the boards of public and private companies.

His board experience includes Udemy, Sprout Social and Carbon Health. He has worked across the venture ecosystem as a founder, general partner, limited partner, direct investor and company builder.

Paul is the founder of Prescient Road, where he advises family offices entering venture capital. His experience includes BuildersVC as Co-founder and General Partner, Roniin LLC as Founder & CEO, Lightbank as General Partner, and Peacock Equity Fund as Founding Partner. Roniin now serves as his investing vehicle. He invests in funds through Bling Ventures, LLC, and in real estate through Flo Holdings.

He double majored in Mathematical Methods in the Social Sciences and Economics at Northwestern University. He splits his time between Siesta Key and Chicago's Gold Coast.
